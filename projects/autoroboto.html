Automated biometric testing for Pixel.

Robotics lab automation for evaluation of Google Pixel biometric face authentication models. I built the trajectory system and led the ROS1→ROS2 migration, deployed on 10 robot labs.

What I work on

  • Trajectory system: Built a trajectory system that uses a robotic arm in combination with lighting and background to search a broad range of setups that expose model vulnerabilities.
  • Capture-frame precision: Improved system precision by mapping robot position and velocity to the exact camera capture frame, which enabled deeper evaluation insights and higher precision attacks on the models.
  • Human motion on the robot: Measured human movements using an IMU and integrated those motions on the robot, broadening the range of authentication modalities the system could evaluate.
  • ROS1 → ROS2 migration: Led the system-wide architecture migration: a threading architecture rewrite and improved motion planning, deployed on 10 robot labs.
